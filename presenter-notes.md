# Walk in the Light of Your Fire: Presenter Notes

Isaiah 50–57 · Come, Follow Me, Oct 5–11

**Timing:** plan for about 22 minutes of real time. Times below run about 24 min, so trim as you go.
**If running long, shorten:** the activity, the 53 discussion, or skip 50:1–2.

## Opening image + title
*Before class*

- Greet people at the door
- Invite someone for the opening prayer and someone for the closing prayer

## Reflection
*0:00–3:00 (3 min)*

- Briefly introduce the topic: Isaiah 50–57
- Open it up: anything from your study this week, or from life
- Transition: "Let's start in Isaiah 50."

## Isaiah 50:1–2
*3:00–5:00 (2 min)*

- Hand/arm in Isaiah = His power
- "Is my hand shortened?" = can anything get in the way of His power? No.
- "I dry up the sea" = the Red Sea, parted by God's power through Moses
- So complete they didn't wade through water; the fish died. His power is thorough.

## Isaiah 50:10–11
*5:00–8:00 (3 min)*

- v10 action words: fear, obey, trust, stay
- Doing the right things (or going through the motions) and still walking in darkness. We've all been there.
- The plan was never perfection; it's mistakes + relying on Him. Don't get stuck on who's to blame.
- v11 is the answer. Sparks = tender mercies, spiritual witnesses, mostly small
- "Compass yourselves" = surround yourself with them
- "Walk in the light" = act. Sometimes it only lights one step, and that's enough.
- Faith isn't knowing; it's hoping, acting, trusting
- "The sparks that YE have kindled": He knows you have some. Don't compare to someone else's fire.
- Optional Q: what does "ye shall lie down in sorrow" mean?

## "Hold fast to what you already know"
*10:15–10:45 (30 sec)*

- Tie to 50:11: "the sparks that ye have kindled"
- You already have some light. Hold to it and walk by it.

## Isaiah 51 highlighting activity
*10:45–13:45 (3 min)*

- Bridge: 50:10 showed action words and what follows. Isaiah 51 is full of that pattern.
- Click "Open the live board" (Reset before class)

## Discussion: covenant pattern
*13:45–15:45 (2 min)*

- Do our part, and His promises become light for the next step
- Transition: "And we don't walk that path alone."

## Isaiah 52:12
*15:45–18:15 (2.5 min)*

- v11 lead-in: depart, be clean, don't rush
- Night-forest story: can only see ahead, unnerving not to know what's behind
- "The LORD will go before you": not a random path. He already walked it.
- "Rear guard": He's got your back, including your past and mistakes
- You may only see one step, but you're not alone

## Isaiah 53:4–5
*18:15–19:45 (1.5 min)*

- After how we treated Him (v3), He bore it anyway
- "Stripes" = the lashes. His injury is our healing.
- We don't deserve Him by justice; His Atonement overcomes even that
- "I don't know exactly how it works, but I know that it works."
